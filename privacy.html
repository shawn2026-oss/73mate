Privacy Policy

73MATE App (iOS and Android) — Last updated: October 12, 2026

Overview

"73MATE" is developed and maintained by Super73 Lab Taiwan. We take your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

In short: 73MATE has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your bike data stays on your device. Your location leaves your device only for the features that need it — weather, map search and route planning — as described below.

Data Collection

We, the developer, do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Financial information
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers

The map component on Android is provided by Google and sends some technical data to Google directly. See "Maps and Navigation" below.

Bluetooth (BLE) Connection

73MATE connects to your Super73 bike via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to read vehicle data and send control commands. All communication occurs directly between your device and your bike. No data is transmitted to any server.

Location Data

73MATE uses your device's location for the speedometer, ride distance, weather and navigation. Speed and ride statistics are calculated on your device and are never uploaded.

While your bike is connected and a ride is in progress, the App keeps using location in the background so the ride is recorded with the screen off. On Android this runs as a foreground service with a visible notification, which you can stop at any time from the notification.

You can deny or revoke the location permission in your device settings. The bike controls keep working without it.

Weather Data

73MATE uses the Open-Meteo API to display weather, sunrise and sunset for where you are. Only your coordinates are sent to retrieve the forecast. No personal information is included in these requests.

Maps and Navigation

When you search for a place or plan a route, the search text, your current location and the destination are sent to the map provider so it can return results:

  • iOS: Apple Maps (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Android: Google Maps Platform — Maps SDK, Places API and Routes API (Google Privacy Policy)

These requests are made only when you use the navigation screen. They are not linked to an account in 73MATE, and we do not receive or store them.

On Android, the map is drawn by Google's Maps SDK. As Google documents, the SDK itself sends Google diagnostic data (such as crash reports and your device model and OS version), a pseudonymous identifier specific to the SDK, and map interactions such as panning and zooming. Google uses this to maintain and improve its services. This data goes to Google, not to us.

Local Data Storage

The App uses your device's local storage to save:

  • Bike settings and preferences (mode, assist level, light)
  • Ride profiles
  • Ride statistics (distance, duration)
  • Vehicle information (serial number, firmware, ODO mileage)
  • Bike photos

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. If you have turned on your device's own backup (iCloud or Google backup), it may be included in that backup under your own account. Deleting the App deletes all local data.

Third-Party Services

We have not added any analytics tools, advertising services, or tracking technologies to this App. The only outside services it contacts are the ones named above: Open-Meteo for weather, and Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps Platform (Android) for maps and navigation, including the diagnostic data Google's Maps SDK collects on Android.

Deleting Your Data

73MATE has no accounts and keeps nothing about you on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the App has stored, delete the App from your device.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised content and date will be posted on this page. We recommend checking this page periodically.
