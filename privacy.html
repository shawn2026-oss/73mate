Privacy Policy

73MATE App — Last updated: March 19, 2026

Overview

"73MATE" is developed and maintained by Super73 Lab Taiwan. We take your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

In short: 73MATE does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All your data stays on your device.

Data Collection

This App does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Financial information
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers

Bluetooth (BLE) Connection

73MATE connects to your Super73 bike via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to read vehicle data and send control commands. All communication occurs directly between your device and your bike. No data is transmitted to any server.

Location Data

73MATE uses your device's location services for ride tracking and navigation features. Location data is processed locally on your device and is not sent to any external server.

Weather Data

73MATE uses the Open-Meteo API to display weather information based on your approximate location. Only coordinate data is sent to retrieve weather forecasts. No personal information is included in these requests.

Local Data Storage

The App uses your device's local storage (UserDefaults and file system) to save:

  • Bike settings and preferences (mode, assist level, light)
  • Ride profiles
  • Ride statistics (distance, duration)
  • Vehicle information (serial number, firmware, ODO mileage)
  • Bike photos

This data remains solely on your device and is not transmitted to any server. Deleting the App will also delete all local data.

Third-Party Services

This App does not use any third-party analytics tools, advertising services, or tracking technologies.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised content and date will be posted on this page. We recommend checking this page periodically.
